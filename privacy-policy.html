Snaply Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

In short: Snaply works on your device. Your screenshots and recordings are never uploaded, and Snaply has no analytics and no ads. If you buy Snaply Pro, the payment provider receives your email and payment details to process the purchase.

What stays on your device

  • Screenshots and your edits are stored locally in your browser (IndexedDB) for the History feature. You can delete them at any time from History or by removing the extension.
  • Screen, tab and camera recordings exist only in memory in the recorder window until you download them. Snaply doesn't keep a copy.
  • Camera and microphone are used only while you are recording, and only after you allow them in Chrome.
  • Settings (your capture and recording preferences) are stored with Chrome's storage and may sync between your own Chrome profiles if you use Chrome Sync.

What Snaply does not do

  • It doesn't upload, sell or share your screenshots, recordings or browsing data.
  • It doesn't use analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • It only reads a page when you start a capture or recording on it.

Purchases (Snaply Pro)

Snaply Pro is sold through ExtensionPay, which uses Stripe to process payments. When you buy or restore Pro, you give your email address and payment details to ExtensionPay and Stripe. Snaply never sees your card details. The extension only receives whether your Pro purchase is active. ExtensionPay and Stripe handle this data under their own privacy policies.

Permissions

Snaply asks for Chrome permissions only to capture the screen or tab you choose, record video and audio you start, save files to your Downloads, copy images to your clipboard, and confirm Pro purchases on extensionpay.com.

Children

Snaply isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated.
